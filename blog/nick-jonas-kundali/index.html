Life events, laid against the periods

Public, dated events. Beside each: the dasha that was running and where Saturn and Jupiter stood, computed for that day. Below each, in its own voice, what the tradition would make of it.

  1. 8 August 2006 The Jonas Brothers' first album is released

    Moon mahadasha, Rahu antardasha (14 Nov 2004 → 15 Nov 2014) · Saturn in Cancer, the 4th house · Jupiter in Libra, the 7th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads work and standing through the 10th, 6th, 2nd, 11th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Rahu sits in the 9th — and to the Moon.

  2. 1 December 2018 Marries Priyanka Chopra

    Mars mahadasha, Mercury antardasha (15 Nov 2014 → 14 Nov 2021) · Saturn in Sagittarius, the 9th house · Jupiter in Scorpio, the 8th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads partnership through the 7th, 5th, 11th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Mercury sits in the 6th — and to the Moon.

  3. 15 January 2022 Daughter Malti Marie is born

    Rahu mahadasha, Rahu antardasha (14 Nov 2021 → 15 Nov 2039) · Saturn in Capricorn, the 10th house · Jupiter in Aquarius, the 11th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads a milestone through the 1st, 10th, 11th, 9th houses. Here, Rahu, the running lord, sits in the 9th; Saturn was crossing the 10th; Jupiter was crossing the 11th — the houses this kind of moment is read through were in play.

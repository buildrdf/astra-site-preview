Life events, laid against the periods

Public, dated events. Beside each: the dasha that was running and where Saturn and Jupiter stood, computed for that day. Below each, in its own voice, what the tradition would make of it.

  1. 1 April 2004 Joins Google

    Rahu mahadasha, Venus antardasha (7 Nov 1989 → 7 Nov 2007) · Saturn in Gemini, the 2nd house · Jupiter in Leo, the 4th house, retrograde

    Interpretation Tradition reads work and standing through the 10th, 6th, 2nd, 11th houses. Here, Venus, the running lord, sits in the 2nd; Saturn was crossing the 2nd — the houses this kind of moment is read through were in play.

  2. 10 August 2015 Becomes chief executive of Google

    Jupiter mahadasha, Ketu antardasha (7 Nov 2007 → 7 Nov 2023) · Saturn in Scorpio, the 7th house · Jupiter in Leo, the 4th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads a milestone through the 1st, 10th, 11th, 9th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Ketu sits in the 3rd — and to the Moon.

  3. 3 December 2019 Becomes chief executive of Alphabet

    Jupiter mahadasha, Moon antardasha (7 Nov 2007 → 7 Nov 2023) · Saturn in Sagittarius, the 8th house · Jupiter in Sagittarius, the 8th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads work and standing through the 10th, 6th, 2nd, 11th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Moon sits in the 1st — and to the Moon.

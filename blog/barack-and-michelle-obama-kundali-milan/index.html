Manglik, compared

Barack Obama: Mars in the 8th from the ascendant — one of the placements tradition names Mangal dosha. Michelle Obama: Mars in the 12th from the Moon — a Mangal dosha placement. Where both charts carry it, tradition reads the two as cancelling each other.

One of the two birth times is unknown, so that chart's Mars is counted from the Moon rather than the ascendant, and the houses of the match cannot be read in full.

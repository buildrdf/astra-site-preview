Manglik, compared

Prince William: Mars in the 10th from the ascendant — not a Mangal dosha placement. Catherine, Princess of Wales: Mars in the 4th from the Moon — a Mangal dosha placement. Where one chart carries it, tradition looks for the cancellations in the other chart before reading it as an obstacle.

One of the two birth times is unknown, so that chart's Mars is counted from the Moon rather than the ascendant, and the houses of the match cannot be read in full.

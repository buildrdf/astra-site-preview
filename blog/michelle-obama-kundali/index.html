Life events, laid against the periods

Public, dated events. Beside each: the dasha that was running and where Saturn and Jupiter stood, computed for that day. Below each, in its own voice, what the tradition would make of it.

  1. 3 October 1992 Marries Barack Obama

    Jupiter mahadasha, Moon antardasha (19 May 1981 → 19 May 1997) · Saturn in Capricorn, the 12th house, retrograde · Jupiter in Virgo, the 8th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads partnership through the 7th, 5th, 11th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Moon sits in the 1st — and to the Moon.

  2. 20 January 2009 Becomes First Lady

    Saturn mahadasha, Moon antardasha (19 May 1997 → 19 May 2016) · Saturn in Leo, the 7th house, retrograde · Jupiter in Capricorn, the 12th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads work and standing through the 10th, 6th, 2nd, 11th houses. The running lords and the slow transits were elsewhere that season; a reader would look to the antardasha lord's own house — Moon sits in the 1st — and to the Moon.

  3. 13 November 2018 Becoming is published

    Mercury mahadasha, Ketu antardasha (19 May 2016 → 19 May 2033) · Saturn in Sagittarius, the 11th house · Jupiter in Scorpio, the 10th house

    Interpretation Tradition reads a milestone through the 1st, 10th, 11th, 9th houses.

What makes it personal

Written from your chart, not assembled from a catalogue.

Every house is read with its lord, its occupants and its aspects together, and the report says which of those it leaned on. The year ahead is built from your dashas and the real transits over your houses, month by month. The remedy plan is ranked for your chart, kept optional, and never sold through fear. Deep technical tables stay in the app, where they can be touched, rather than padding the reading.

Questions

Before you get it

How do I buy it?

Inside the Astra app, on iPhone or Android. Open your chart, choose the Complete Kundali, and pay through the store you use. There is no web checkout.

How is it different from the free report?

The free report shows what is in the chart. The Complete Kundali explains it: strength scores, aspects and conjunctions, yoga activation, precise timing for career, money and marriage, a twelve-month forecast, a decision calendar, a remedy plan and a 90-day action plan.

Does it go out of date?

The chart never changes, and the report is dated the day it was cast. The year-ahead pages cover the twelve months from that date; the app keeps daily guidance current after that.

Can I get one for someone else?

Yes. Add their birth details as a person in the app and buy the report for that chart.

What about refunds?

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, and refunds follow that store's policy.

What makes it personal

Two charts, one relationship.

The Ashtakoota is scored koota by koota, with what each one means and what it cannot tell you. Manglik is compared across both charts, with the cancellations that apply. Timing uses both partners' dashas and the transits over both charts, so the twelve-month weather belongs to the relationship, not to one of you.

Questions

Before you get it

Do we both need to be on Astra?

No. You enter your partner's birth details as a person in the app; the report is cast for the two charts.

Do you need both birth times?

Yes, for the full reading. The Moon-based kootas can be scored from dates alone, but the charts, the houses and the timing need the times.

What does the score mean?

The Ashtakoota gives up to 36 points across eight kootas. The report shows each koota, what it measures and its limits, so the number is understood rather than feared.

Can it be done for a marriage that has already happened?

Yes. It reads the two charts as they are, whenever you were married.

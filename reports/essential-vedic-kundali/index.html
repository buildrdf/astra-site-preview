What makes it personal

Cast for your minute and your place, not your sign.

A sun-sign horoscope is written for a twelfth of the world. This report is computed from the exact minute and place of your birth: the ascendant, the Moon's nakshatra, the navamsha and the Mahadasha timeline all follow from it, and every one of them is dated. The prose beside each chart is written from those placements, in the two voices the app uses: the guidance in full, and under it, quietly, what it was read from.

Questions

Before you get it

Do I need the app to read it?

No. The report arrives by email as a PDF. Sign in to the app with the same email and it is already waiting there, and the app keeps the timing alive after the PDF is read.

Why do you need my time of birth?

The ascendant changes sign about every two hours, and the houses follow it. Without the time the chart can only be a wheel of signs. If you know the time only roughly, use the nearest you have; the report states the birth details it was cast from.

Which system does it use?

The sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa, the Vimshottari dasha, and the North Indian chart. The same engine runs the app.

What happens to my details?

They are kept only to cast and send the report, and to place it in the app when you sign in with the same email. Every email carries a link to set the report aside if it was not you who asked.

Is this a prediction?

No. It shows what is in the chart and what the tradition associates with it. It never says something will happen.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

The app

Where does Astra run?

On iPhone and Android. The free Kundali is cast on this site and is already in the app when you sign in with the same email.

What does it need from me?

Your date, time and place of birth, and an email to sign in with. The time matters: the ascendant changes sign about every two hours.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use the nearest time you have. The chart states the details it was cast from. The Moon sign and the dashas depend far less on the time than the ascendant does.

Is Ask Astra a real astrologer?

It is a guide that answers from the verified facts of your chart, computed by the engine, in the tradition's terms. It is not allowed to invent a placement or a date, and it never predicts an outcome.

Does Astra predict the future?

No. It says what the tradition associates with a placement or a period and shows the working. Nothing on Astra is a forecast, and nothing is medical, legal or financial advice.

The reports

What is in the free report?

Sixteen pages: your birth details and Panchang, the birth, Moon and navamsha charts, planetary positions, the Mahadasha timeline and current period, your Kundali at a glance, and previews of career, money, love and wellbeing.

How do I get the Complete Kundali or the Kundali Milan?

Inside the app, on iPhone or Android. Open your chart, choose the report, and pay through the store you use. There is no web checkout.

How is the report delivered?

As a PDF, A4, by email, and saved in the app. The free report arrives the moment it is cast; the paid ones are ready within minutes of purchase.

What about refunds?

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, and refunds follow that store's policy.

Does a report go out of date?

The chart never changes. A report is dated the day it was cast, and its year-ahead pages cover the twelve months from then; the app keeps daily guidance current after that.

Your data

What happens to my birth details?

They are used to cast your chart and report, and kept in your account so the report is there when you sign in. Nobody else can see them. Deleting your account deletes them.

Who else sees my questions to Ask Astra?

Your question and the chart facts it needs are sent to an AI provider to compose the answer; the provider does not receive your email address. See the privacy page.

The tradition

Traditionally a grahan falls where Rahu or Ketu stand, and its sign and nakshatra — here Capricorn and Dhanishta — are read as the matters brought into the open, most for those whose Moon or ascendant is there. The observances around an eclipse are customs, offered without a promised outcome. Which house it falls in is what makes it yours; Astra shows it in your own chart.

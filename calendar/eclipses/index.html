Eclipse Calendar

Surya and Chandra Grahan: every eclipse of 2026 and 2027, with the time of greatest eclipse in IST, where it is seen, and the sign and nakshatra it falls in.

Astronomy from NASA's eclipse canon, to be verified before launch · positions computed by Astra's engine

2026

  1. 17 February 2026 at 5:42 pm ISTAnnular solar eclipse of 17 February 2026

    annular solar · the Moon in Aquarius, Dhanishta

    Annular only from Antarctica; partial across southern Africa, the southern tip of South America and the far south of the Indian Ocean. Not visible from India.

  2. 3 March 2026 at 5:04 pm ISTTotal lunar eclipse of 3 March 2026

    total lunar · the Moon in Leo, Purva Phalguni

    Total across the Pacific, the Americas and East Asia. In India the Moon rises already in eclipse on the evening of 3 March, so the end of totality and the partial phases are seen from moonrise.

  3. 12 August 2026 at 11:16 pm ISTTotal solar eclipse of 12 August 2026

    total solar · the Moon in Cancer, Ashlesha

    Totality crosses the Arctic, Greenland, Iceland and northern Spain at sunset; partial across Europe, north-west Africa and northern North America. Not visible from India.

  4. 28 August 2026 at 9:43 am ISTPartial lunar eclipse of 28 August 2026

    partial lunar · the Moon in Aquarius, Shatabhisha

    Partial across the Americas, Europe, Africa and the Pacific. In India the Moon sets before the eclipse begins, so it is not seen.

2027

  1. 6 February 2027 at 9:30 pm ISTAnnular solar eclipse of 6 February 2027

    annular solar · the Moon in Capricorn, Dhanishta

    Annular across the southern Pacific, Chile, Argentina and the South Atlantic; partial across South America and West Africa. Not visible from India.

  2. 21 February 2027 at 4:43 am ISTPenumbral lunar eclipse of 20 February 2027

    penumbral lunar · the Moon in Leo, Magha

    A faint penumbral shading, visible where the Moon is up across the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia; from India, in the early hours of 21 February.

  3. 2 August 2027 at 3:37 pm ISTTotal solar eclipse of 2 August 2027

    total solar · the Moon in Cancer, Pushya

    A long totality across southern Spain, North Africa, Egypt and the Arabian Peninsula; partial across Europe, Africa and western and southern Asia, including much of India in the afternoon.

  4. 17 August 2027 at 12:44 pm ISTPenumbral lunar eclipse of 17 August 2027

    penumbral lunar · the Moon in Capricorn, Dhanishta

    A faint penumbral shading over the Pacific, Australia and East Asia; from India the Moon has set.

How to read an eclipse

An eclipse is geometry: the Moon's shadow on the Earth, or the Earth's on the Moon. Whether you see it depends only on where you stand. The tradition reads a grahan by the sign and nakshatra it falls in, and by the house that sign occupies in your own chart. How transits are read.

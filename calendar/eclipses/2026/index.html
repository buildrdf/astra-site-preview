Eclipses in 2026

4 eclipses, each with the time of greatest eclipse in IST and where it is seen.

Astronomy from NASA's eclipse canon, to be verified before launch

  1. 17 February 2026 at 5:42 pm ISTAnnular solar eclipse of 17 February 2026

    annular solar · the Moon in Aquarius, Dhanishta

    Annular only from Antarctica; partial across southern Africa, the southern tip of South America and the far south of the Indian Ocean. Not visible from India.

  2. 3 March 2026 at 5:04 pm ISTTotal lunar eclipse of 3 March 2026

    total lunar · the Moon in Leo, Purva Phalguni

    Total across the Pacific, the Americas and East Asia. In India the Moon rises already in eclipse on the evening of 3 March, so the end of totality and the partial phases are seen from moonrise.

  3. 12 August 2026 at 11:16 pm ISTTotal solar eclipse of 12 August 2026

    total solar · the Moon in Cancer, Ashlesha

    Totality crosses the Arctic, Greenland, Iceland and northern Spain at sunset; partial across Europe, north-west Africa and northern North America. Not visible from India.

  4. 28 August 2026 at 9:43 am ISTPartial lunar eclipse of 28 August 2026

    partial lunar · the Moon in Aquarius, Shatabhisha

    Partial across the Americas, Europe, Africa and the Pacific. In India the Moon sets before the eclipse begins, so it is not seen.

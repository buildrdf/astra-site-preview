How Astra works

Calculated, then explained.

The methods and assumptions behind every chart, period and reading, so you can check them.

The zodiac

Astra uses the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa, the standard of Indian government almanacs. A placement here can differ from a Western (tropical) chart by about twenty-four degrees; that difference is the ayanamsa. The lesson Lahiri ayanamsa explains it.

The chart

Planetary longitudes are computed by Astra's own ephemeris from the birth instant in Universal Time, which the app derives from your local birth time and place. The ascendant is computed for the place of birth; houses follow the whole-sign convention drawn in the North Indian style, the ascendant's sign being the first house. Rahu and Ketu are the mean lunar nodes.

Periods and timing

Dashas follow the Vimshottari system: a 120-year cycle counted from the Moon's nakshatra at birth, with a year of 365.25 days. Mahadasha, antardasha and pratyantardasha are all computed from that one starting point. Transits are read against the natal houses, and Sade Sati from Saturn's actual sign entries, retrograde re-entries included.

The Panchang

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and vara are computed for the day and the place from the Sun's and Moon's positions; sunrise and sunset are computed for the place.

Interpretation

The tradition's associations are written in two voices: the guidance, in plain language, and under it the working it was read from. Astra's guide, Ask Astra, answers from verified chart facts computed by the engine and is not allowed to invent a position, a date or a period; where a fact is not in view it says so. Nothing is presented as certain, and nothing is presented as medical, legal or financial advice.

What can be wrong

A birth time that is off by a few minutes can move the ascendant near a sign boundary; the report states the details it was cast from so you can check them. Ephemeris positions are accurate to well under a degree for the twentieth and twenty-first centuries. If you find an error, tell us; corrections are noted on the page they affect.

What Astra is, and is not

Astra calculates your birth chart from the birth details you give it and explains it within the framework of Vedic astrology. Everything it says is traditional interpretation, offered for reflection and learning. It is not medical, mental-health, legal, financial or safety advice, it does not predict outcomes, and no decision should rest on it alone.

Your account and your data

  • You sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email. We store your email address to recognise you.
  • The birth details you enter for yourself and for the people you add, the life events you choose to save, and your preferences are stored in your own account. No other user can see them.
  • When you ask Guide a question, your question and the chart facts it needs are sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to compose the answer. Voice conversations are carried by OpenAI. These providers process the data to answer you and do not receive your email address.
  • The production site uses Google Analytics to count visits and screens. No advertising is shown and your data is never sold.
  • You can remove people, events and your chart inside the app. To delete your account and everything in it, open Settings in the You tab and choose Delete my account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

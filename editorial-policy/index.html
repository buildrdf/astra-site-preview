Editorial policy

Sources, authorship, review and corrections.

The rules every page on this site is written under.

Computed before written

Every chart, date, dasha and transit on this site is computed by Astra's engine at the time the page is built, and the page says when. A writer decides where a computed piece goes and what to say about it; a writer never draws a chart or types a date.

Sources

Lessons draw on the classical texts of Vedic astrology and their standard modern commentaries. Famous Kundali pages state their birth details with a confidence rating: AA for a birth record, A for a time given by the person or their family, B for a biography, C for a widely quoted but unverified time, and X where no time is published, in which case the chart is drawn from the Moon and says so. Public life events are dated from public records. Eclipse dates and times follow NASA's eclipse canon. Every such entry is checked against its primary source before publication; until then the page carries a note.

Authorship and review

Articles are written by Reality Distortion Field and reviewed for astrological accuracy and for the site's stance before they are published. Interpretation is always labelled as interpretation and kept apart from the computed facts beside it.

Corrections

If a date, a placement or a fact is wrong, write to us. Corrections are made on the page and the page's updated date changes; a material correction is noted at the foot of the page.

What we do not publish

Predictions of death, disaster or the outcome of a pregnancy; claims that a period caused an event; remedies sold through fear; and any number or claim we cannot show the working for.
